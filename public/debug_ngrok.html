FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I publish a book if I have no experience?

You don't need prior experience. We guide you through the entire process from idea validation to publishing and marketing, so you don't feel lost at any stage.

How much does it cost to publish a book?

Costs vary depending on services like writing, editing, and marketing. We provide a customized plan based on your goals, so you only pay for what you actually need.

Can you help if I only have a rough idea and not a manuscript?

Yes. Our ghostwriting service helps turn your idea into a professionally written book ready for publishing.

Why isn't my self-published book getting sales?

Most books fail due to poor positioning, weak visibility, or lack of marketing. We fix this by planning targeted strategies, platform optimization, and audience-focused Marketing.

How long does it take to publish and launch a book?

It depends on the project, but most books are completed and launched within a few weeks to a few months.

Do I need an author website to succeed?

If you want long-term success and credibility, yes. An author website helps you build authority, collect leads, and promote your work effectively.

Can you convert my book into an audiobook?

Yes. We provide full audiobook production including narration, editing, and distribution setup.

What makes your service different from freelancers or platforms?

We don't just deliver tasks, we provide a complete strategy, execution, and growth system focused on real results, not just publishing.
